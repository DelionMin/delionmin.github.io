This project explored whether global macroeconomic indicators could improve multi-stock price forecasting. I helped propose and implement a model that combines FiLM-based global conditioning with stock-wise self-attention, and I presented the work.

We studied sensitivity across attention configurations, model dimensions, input-window sizes, and prediction horizons. The project achieved **1st place** among teams in the December 2025 course evaluation at the University of Seoul.
