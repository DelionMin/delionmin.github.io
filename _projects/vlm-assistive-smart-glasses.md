In December 2025, I led a team that developed VLM-based assistive smart glasses for visually impaired users. The system captures images on a wearable device, sends them to a server, and returns generated responses in real time.

I developed the server-side inference pipeline around the Gemini API and integrated image capture, device-server communication, and VLM inference into a complete working prototype. The project won **1st place** in the University of Seoul competition.
