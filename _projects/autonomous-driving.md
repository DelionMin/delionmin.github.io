As team leader, I developed a ROS2-based autonomous-driving framework that integrated camera and LiDAR perception with vehicle-control modules.

The system used a classical computer-vision lane-following pipeline with Hough-transform-based lane detection. For lane-change decisions, it combined YOLO-based obstacle and vehicle detection with LiDAR-based inter-vehicle distance estimation. Our team placed **9th out of 82 teams** in the June 2025 final competition.
